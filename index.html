    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything people ask us before picking up their pass. Still unsure? Our helpline is open 10 AM – 7 PM.

    What is the SITI Puja Pass 2026?

    It is a single pass that lets your family walk into 45 popular Durga Puja pandals across Kolkata — no separate ticket at any of them. One pass admits four people.

    How many people can enter on one pass?

    Four. If you are a larger group, add more passes to the same booking — up to 10 passes, admitting 40 people, can go through in one order.

    When is the pass valid?

    From 14 October to 21 October 2026. The same pass works on every one of those days, so you can spread your pandal hopping across the week rather than doing it all in one evening. It admits four people, and only four.

    What does ₹1,499 include?

    The full price of one pass, with 18% GST already inside it — nothing is added at checkout. The only extra is courier delivery at a flat ₹99 per order, and that disappears if you choose self pickup.

    Which pandals does the pass cover?

    All 45 are listed on this page, spread from Bagbazar and Shyambazar in the north to Behala and Tollygunge in the south. See the full list.

    How do I receive my pass after booking?

    Two ways, and you pick one on the booking form. Courier brings it to your address for a flat ₹99, or you collect it free from any of our eight pickup centres.

    Where are the pickup centres?

    There are eight counters across the city, and you choose the one nearest you while booking. See all eight centres and their timings.

    How will I know my booking is confirmed?

    A confirmation goes to your email as soon as the booking is placed, and our team calls you on the number you gave us. You can also confirm instantly on WhatsApp from the button that appears after you submit the form.

    Can I book more than 10 passes?

    The form takes up to 10 passes in one go. For anything larger — an office group, a housing society, a corporate block booking — talk to us on the helpline or message us on WhatsApp and we will set it up for you.
